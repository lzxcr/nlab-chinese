## Definition

 
###### Definition 

An **(finitary) essentially algebraic $(\infty,1)$-theory** is an [(∞,1)-category](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/%28%E2%88%9E%2C1%29-category) $T$ with (finite) [(∞,1)-limit](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/%28%E2%88%9E%2C1%29-limit)s.

An **algebra** over an essentially algebraic $(\infty,1)$-theory in some [(∞,1)-topos](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/%28%E2%88%9E%2C1%29-topos) $\mathcal{X}$ is a (finite) $(\infty,1)$-limit preserving [(∞,1)-functor](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/%28%E2%88%9E%2C1%29-functor)

$$
  A : T \to \mathcal{X}
  \,.
$$



## Properties

* An ordinary [essentially algebraic theory](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/essentially%20algebraic%20theory) is a 0-[truncated](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/truncated) essentially algebraic $(\infty,1)$-theory.

* If $X$ is an [(∞,1)-topos](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/%28%E2%88%9E%2C1%29-topos), $T$-algebras in $\mathcal{X}$ correspond to left exact left adjoints $PSh(T) \to \mathcal{X}$ under the equivalence $\Fun^L(PSh(T), \mathcal{X}) \to \Fun(T, \mathcal{X})$

## Examples

* A [geometry (for structured (∞,1)-toposes)](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/geometry%20%28for%20structured%20%28%E2%88%9E%2C1%29-toposes%29) is an essentially algebraic $(\infty,1)$-theory.
