## 想法

有时被称为 **Rehren duality** 的是 [局部可观测网](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/causally-local-net-of-observables) 与它们在边界上的限制之间的关系。它最初被声称是 [CFT](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/AdS-CFT-correspondence) 对偶的一个玩具模型的形式化，但在那个语境中，域论并非简单地被限制在它们的边界上（相反，[态](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/state) 被映射到 [相关函数](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/correlator)）。

## Idea

What is sometimes called __Rehren duality__ is a relation between [local nets of observables](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/local%20nets%20of%20observables) and their restriction to a boundary. It was originally claimed to be a formalization of a toy model of [AdS/CFT](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/AdS%2FCFT) duality, but in that context field theories are not simply restricted to their boundaries (instead [state](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/state)s are mapped to [correlator](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/correlator)s).
